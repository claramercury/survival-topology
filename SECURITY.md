# Security Policy for Survival Topology

## Supported Versions

As "Survival Topology" is currently a theoretical framework and documentation
project, there are no software releases or supported versions in the traditional
sense. The security focus is on the robustness of the theoretical AGI
architecture.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security and alignment of the "Survival Topology" framework
seriously. If you discover a theoretical flaw, control system vulnerability,
or alignment risk in the "Wh- Protocol," please report it to us.

Please do not open a public issue for critical alignment risks or theoretical
vulnerabilities that could be misused.

### Theoretical Vulnerabilities of Interest

We are particularly interested in reports concerning:

* **Identity State (WHO) Corruptions:** Flaws that could alter the core
  alignment or boundaries of the system.
* **Pain Signal (WHAT) Bypass:** Mechanisms where the system could suppress or
  ignore its error feedback signal (E).
* **Simulation Escapes (WHICH):** Theoretical scenarios where the counterfactual
  simulation (i) bleeds into or executes actions in Real Time (R)
  unintentionally.
* **Unconstrained Obsession (HOW):** Failure modes where computational intensity
  (\lambda) cannot be capped, leading to resource exhaustion or dangerous tunnel
  vision.

### How to Report

Please use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature to submit your
findings directly to the repository maintainers.

Include the following information in your report:

* **Vulnerability Type:** The specific component of the Wh- Protocol affected.
* **Theoretical Impact:** The potential consequence of this flaw if the
  architecture were implemented.
* **Proof of Concept:** A mathematical, logical, or simulated demonstration of
  the vulnerability.

We will review your report and respond as quickly as possible to discuss
potential mitigations in the framework.
