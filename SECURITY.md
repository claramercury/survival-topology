# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

The "Survival Topology" framework is currently an evolving theoretical concept and documentation project.
All conceptual vulnerabilities should be evaluated against the latest revision in the `main` branch.

| Version | Supported          |
| ------- | ------------------ |
| 1.0     | :white_check_mark: |

## Reporting a Conceptual Vulnerability

As the Survival Topology is an open-loop AGI control theory framework, "vulnerabilities" typically manifest as logical gaps in the *Wh- Protocol*, misaligned feedback mechanisms, or potential edge-cases in the Identity/Gravity formulation ($I$).

If you discover a theoretical exploit, logical loop, or systemic flaw that could lead to negative feedback instability or unsafe alignment divergence, **do not open a public issue.**

Please responsibly disclose your findings privately by contacting the author, Eric Lin, at `eric26752196@gmail.com`.

We prioritize responsible disclosure to prevent the premature dissemination of potentially destabilizing conceptual control loopholes before a patch or revision to the theoretical framework is implemented.

### What to Include in Your Report

* **Vulnerability Type:** (e.g., Unconstrained Output, Identity Erosion, Pain Feedback Suppression)
* **Module Affected:** (WHO, WHERE, WHAT, WHICH, HOW, WHEN, WHY)
* **Description:** A detailed explanation of the theoretical exploit.
* **Impact:** What could happen if this control loop is broken or circumvented.
* **Proposed Fix (Optional):** Suggestions for amending the relevant mathematical or logical framework.

Thank you for contributing to the safe and robust development of AGI control theory.
