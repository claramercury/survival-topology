# Security Policy for Survival Topology

Thank you for your interest in the security of the Survival Topology framework. As this project explores a theoretical control theory framework for General Intelligence (the Wh- Protocol), ensuring the integrity and security of its concepts and any associated implementations is of paramount importance.

## Supported Versions

Currently, as the project is in the theoretical draft phase (Version 1.0), all discussions, conceptual documentation, and theoretical implementations are under active review.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover any security-related vulnerabilities, flaws in the conceptual architecture, or potential risks in the Wh- Protocol, we mandate **private responsible disclosure**.

Please **DO NOT** open public issues or pull requests to report vulnerabilities or theoretical exploits. Exposing these details publicly can pose risks to the framework's integrity or any early implementations based on it.

Instead, please report all security concerns directly and privately to the project author:

**Contact Email:** [eric26752196@gmail.com](mailto:eric26752196@gmail.com)

### What to Include in Your Report

When reporting a vulnerability, please provide the following details:

* A description of the vulnerability, theoretical flaw, or risk.
* Steps or a conceptual proof (e.g., mathematical or logical reasoning) to reproduce the issue.
* The potential impact or "Pain" ($E$) on the system's identity state ($I$).
* Any proposed mitigations or "Feedback" loops to correct the error.

We take all security reports seriously and will acknowledge your email promptly. We will work with you to understand the issue, verify it within the context of the Survival Topology architecture, and develop a secure resolution before any public disclosure.

Thank you for helping keep the Survival Topology framework safe and robust.
