# Security Policy for Survival Topology

Thank you for your interest in the security of the Survival Topology project. We take security vulnerabilities very seriously.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

As this project focuses on conceptual and architectural frameworks regarding the "Wh- Protocol," potential vulnerabilities may arise in implementations or structural concepts.

If you discover a potential vulnerability within the Survival Topology documentation, or any future reference implementations, please follow our responsible disclosure process.

**Do NOT open a public issue.** This exposes the vulnerability before we can provide a fix, placing any potential active implementations at risk.

Instead, please privately report the vulnerability by emailing the author directly:

**Email:** eric26752196@gmail.com

### What to Include in Your Report

When submitting your vulnerability report, please include:

1. **Description:** A detailed description of the vulnerability.
2. **Impact:** The potential risk and impact of the vulnerability being exploited.
3. **Reproduction:** Steps, proofs of concept (PoCs), or scripts to reproduce the issue.
4. **Suggested Fix:** (Optional) Any suggestions on how to remediate the vulnerability.

We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours and provide updates as we work to address the issue.
