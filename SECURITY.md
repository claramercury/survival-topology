# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Security is a top priority for the "Survival Topology" project. We appreciate the community's efforts in identifying and reporting any potential security vulnerabilities.

### Responsible Disclosure

To ensure the safety and integrity of the project, we ask that you adhere to the following responsible disclosure guidelines:

1. **Private Reporting:** Do not open public issues or pull requests for security vulnerabilities. All security-related concerns must be reported privately.
2. **Contact:** Please email the project author, Eric Lin, directly at **eric26752196@gmail.com**.
3. **Information Required:** In your email, please provide a detailed description of the vulnerability, including:
   - The nature of the vulnerability (e.g., specific issue in documentation or theoretical model).
   - Steps to reproduce the issue or a proof-of-concept.
   - The potential impact of the vulnerability.
4. **Resolution:** We will acknowledge your report as quickly as possible, usually within 48 hours. We will keep you updated on our progress as we investigate and develop a fix.

Thank you for helping to keep the "Survival Topology" project secure.
