# Security Policy for Survival Topology

## Supported Versions

The Survival Topology project is primarily a theoretical framework and documentation repository. Security considerations mainly revolve around the integrity of the conceptual models, documentation, and any associated demonstration scripts.

As this is a living document detailing the "Wh- Protocol" and related concepts, we currently provide security support for the latest iteration of the main branch.

| Version | Supported          |
| ------- | ------------------ |
| Main    | :white_check_mark: |
| Drafts  | :x:                |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Security is a critical aspect of artificial general intelligence (AGI) alignment and control systems. We take any potential vulnerabilities in the Survival Topology framework, its mathematical models, or its documentation very seriously.

If you discover a security vulnerability or a conceptual flaw that could lead to misinterpretation or misuse of the framework, please follow these steps for private responsible disclosure:

1. **Do not open a public issue.** Exposing vulnerabilities publicly before they are addressed can pose significant risks.
2. **Contact the Author:** Please report your findings directly to Eric Lin via email at: **eric26752196@gmail.com**.
3. **Provide Details:** In your email, please include:
    * A clear description of the vulnerability or conceptual flaw.
    * Steps to reproduce the issue (if applicable, e.g., in the simulation scripts).
    * The potential impact of the vulnerability.
    * Any suggested mitigations or corrections.

We aim to acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours and will keep you informed of our progress in addressing the issue. We appreciate your efforts to help secure the "Soul" of AGI.
