# Security Policy for Survival Topology

The **Survival Topology** project conceptualizes a control theory framework for General Intelligence (the "Wh- Protocol"). We take the security and robustness of these theoretical models seriously, even in their conceptual stages.

## Scope

This policy covers the conceptual architecture, equations, and simulated implementations described within the 'Survival Topology' documentation. Although this branch currently houses theoretical documents rather than executable production code, the principles discussed touch upon critical AGI safety considerations.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you identify a vulnerability in the proposed architecture, a flaw in the control loop logic that could lead to unconstrained behavior, or any other security concern related to the theoretical implementation, we urge you to practice private, responsible disclosure.

**Please do NOT open a public issue.**

Instead, please report any security or safety concerns directly to the author:

* **Contact Email:** <eric26752196@gmail.com>

### What to Include in Your Report

When contacting the author, please include:

* A description of the vulnerability or theoretical flaw.
* The specific components of the Wh- Protocol affected.
* A hypothetical exploit scenario or proof-of-concept (if applicable).
* Any suggestions for mitigation or architectural revision.

We will review reports promptly and collaborate with you to address valid concerns.
